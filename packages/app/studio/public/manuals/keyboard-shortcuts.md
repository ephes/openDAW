# Keyboard Shortcuts

Most shortcuts can be listed and customized in the [Shortcut Manager](action://open-preferences).

## Modifier Keys

### General

* Hold Shift and click to toggle selection (add/remove)
* Hold Shift and drag selection rectangle to invert selection

### Timeline Regions

* Hold ⌘ and drag to copy a region / clip
* Hold ⌘+Shift and drag to copy a region / clip as mirrored
* Hold Shift and scroll wheel to zoom horizontally
* Hold ⌥ and click to cut a region
* Hold ⌥+Shift and click to cut a region without consolidating

### Notes

* Hold ⌘ and drag to copy notes

### Note Properties (Velocity/Expression)

* Hold ⌘ and drag to draw values
* Hold ⌥ and drag to draw a line

### Automation

* Hold ⌘ and drag to copy points
* Hold ⌥ while dragging to freeze value (move position only)
* Hold Shift and click on curve to create a new point
* Hold Shift while dragging to disable value snapping

### Value Dragging (Knobs, Faders)

* Hold Shift to disable snapping
* Hold ⌥ for fine control

### Warp Markers

* Hold Shift to disable snapping

### Playfield Sample Pads

* Hold ⌥ and drag to copy a sample to another pad
## Device clipboard

Device Cut copies the admitted selection and removes its copyable effects.
Protected effects and instruments stay installed; Cut is unavailable for an
instrument alone. Copy remains available for copyable instruments.
Pasting master output content is one undoable edit. If installation fails, the
previous master tracks and effects are restored.
